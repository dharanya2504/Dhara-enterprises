Three steps. No machine. No waiting.

Great coffee shouldn't need equipment or patience. Marsio is made for the moments in between.

1

Spoon it in

Add a spoonful of Marsio to your favourite cup. Use more for a stronger cup, less for a gentler one.

2

Pour hot water

Or hot milk, if you like it creamy. The powder is made to dissolve smoothly.

3

Stir and sip

That's it. A full, aromatic cup, ready before the phone finishes buzzing.
